## 🧠 Before You Buy Solar, Understand Your Energy Requirements

## ⚡ How Can Solar Reduce Your Electricity Bill?

Solar panels generate electricity when sunlight is available.

When your home uses that electricity directly, you may need to purchase less electricity from the grid.

This can be especially useful when your household consumes significant amounts of electricity during daylight hours.

For example, appliances and equipment such as:

✔ Pool pumps

✔ Geysers

✔ Washing machines

✔ Refrigeration

✔ Household appliances

can potentially make use of solar generation while the panels are producing electricity.

The more effectively your household can use the electricity generated by the solar system, the greater the potential value of that energy.

This is why **system design matters**.

Simply installing as many solar panels as possible does not automatically create the best result.

## 🔋 Do You Need Solar Batteries?

Solar panels and batteries have different functions.

**Solar panels generate electricity.**

**Batteries store electricity for later use.**

Battery storage can be useful when you want to use solar-generated electricity during the evening or keep selected appliances operating when grid power is unavailable.

However, adding batteries can significantly increase the upfront cost of a solar installation.

So, does every homeowner need a large battery?

**No.**

The right battery capacity depends on what you want your solar system to accomplish.

If your primary goal is reducing daytime electricity consumption, you may require a different system configuration from a homeowner who wants substantial backup power during outages.

## 🚨 Will Solar Work During Load Shedding?

This is one of the most important things homeowners should understand before purchasing solar.

**Solar panels alone do not automatically provide backup electricity when the grid goes down.**

A system designed for backup operation may require:

✔ A suitable inverter

✔ Battery storage

✔ Correct electrical configuration

✔ Appropriate backup circuits

The exact requirements depend on which appliances you want to keep operating and how much backup power you require.

For example, one homeowner may only want to keep essential lights, Wi-Fi, refrigeration and security equipment operating.

Another may want to support a much larger portion of the household.

These requirements can result in very different system designs.

This is why professional solar system design and commissioning are important.

## 📊 What Size Solar System Does Your Home Need?

There is no universal "best" solar system size.

A **5kW solar system** may be suitable for one household but insufficient for another.

Likewise, installing a larger system doesn't automatically mean you'll receive better value.

The correct starting point is your household's electricity consumption.

Ask:

✔ How much electricity does the household use each month?

✔ When is electricity consumption highest?

✔ How much electricity is used during daylight hours?

✔ How much backup power is required?

✔ How much suitable roof space is available?

✔ Is the roof affected by shading?

Once these factors are understood, the appropriate combination of solar panels, inverter and battery storage can be considered.

## ☀️ Does Solar Work on Cloudy Days?

Solar panels rely on sunlight to generate electricity.

However, the amount of electricity produced can vary depending on available sunlight and environmental conditions.

This is another reason why system design should be based on realistic energy requirements rather than simply assuming that the system will produce the same amount of electricity every day.

A properly designed system takes the property's conditions and expected energy usage into account.

## 📈 How Long Does Solar Take to Pay for Itself?

This is where **solar ROI** becomes important.

The payback period is essentially the amount of time required for accumulated electricity savings to recover the initial investment.

For example:

**System cost:** R200,000

**Average monthly electricity savings:** R2,500

**Simplified payback calculation:**

`R200,000 ÷ R2,500 = 80 months`

That's approximately **6.7 years**.

However, this is only a simplified example.

Real-world results can be affected by:

✔ Changes in electricity tariffs

✔ Household electricity consumption

✔ Solar system performance

✔ Maintenance

✔ Financing costs

✔ Battery replacement

For this reason, a solar payback calculation should be treated as an estimate rather than a guaranteed result.

## 🏠 When Does Residential Solar Make Sense?

Solar can make particular financial and practical sense for homeowners who have relatively high electricity consumption, suitable roof space and a long-term intention to remain at the property.

It may be worth investigating solar if your household:

✔ Has high monthly electricity bills

✔ Uses significant amounts of electricity during the day

✔ Wants greater protection against rising electricity costs

✔ Requires backup power for essential appliances

✔ Has suitable roof space

✔ Plans to remain in the property for several years

✔ Wants to reduce dependence on the electricity grid

If electricity consumption is very low or you expect to move soon, the financial calculation may be different.

The important thing is to assess your individual circumstances.
